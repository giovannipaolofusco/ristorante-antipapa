About us

A place in the old town of Fondi, opened by people who had neither the right age nor the right CV.

We didn't have the right age, or the right CV. We had a place in the old town of Fondi, an idea about cooking, and no wish to do it the way it's usually done.

We wouldn't know how to describe it the way a food critic would. All we know is the plate always comes back empty.

We opened on 31 July 2024, at nine in the evening. Since then the menu has changed almost entirely at least once: four dishes survive from that first year, and we've reworked three of them anyway. That isn't inconsistency — we follow the season, and we get bored quickly.

Anti what, exactly

The name isn't a dig at anyone. It's the shortest way we found to say how we work: Anti Expert, because we don't lecture anybody; Anti Slow, because a table works when the food actually arrives; Anti Routine, because the menu keeps changing; Anti Heat, which in a Fondi summer counts as a political position.

How we eat here

We put the dishes in the middle of the table. It isn't a rule and nobody will make you share, but it's how we built the menu: portions that make sense on their own and rather more sense together.

We won't make long speeches about ingredients: where the supplier matters, their name is printed next to the dish on the menu.
