Privacy Policy

What data we collect

This website collects personal data in one case only: when you voluntarily complete the contact form. The data collected is the data you enter yourself — your name, email address, telephone number where given, and the content of your message.

We do not collect data through analytics tools, we do not profile visitors, and we do not use profiling or marketing cookies.

Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry (Article 6(1)(b) of the Regulation: pre-contractual measures taken at your request, or Article 6(1)(a), consent, where the enquiry is not contractual in nature). Providing the data is optional, but without the data marked as required we cannot reply to you.
  • Security of the website and of the infrastructure (Article 6(1)(f): the legitimate interest of the Data Controller in keeping the service running and protected against abuse). This purpose covers the technical logs automatically recorded by the hosting provider.

How long we keep it

Messages sent through the contact form are kept for a maximum of 12 months from the date they are received, after which they are deleted. Technical infrastructure logs follow the retention periods of the hosting provider indicated below.

Who it may be disclosed to

The data is neither disseminated nor transferred to third parties for commercial purposes. It is processed by the Data Controller's authorised personnel and by the hosting provider, acting as data processor.

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (2325 3rd Street, Suite 296, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which provides the web hosting service and the technical handling of the contact forms.
