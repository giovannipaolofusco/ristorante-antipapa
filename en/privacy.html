Privacy Policy

What data we collect

This website collects personal data in one case only: when you voluntarily complete the contact form. The data collected is the data you enter yourself — your name, email address, telephone number where given, and the content of your message.

We do not collect data through analytics tools, we do not profile visitors, and we do not use profiling or marketing cookies.

Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry (Article 6(1)(b) of the Regulation: pre-contractual measures taken at your request, or Article 6(1)(a), consent, where the enquiry is not contractual in nature). Providing the data is optional: you are free not to write to us. Your name, email address and the text of your message are however necessary for a reply to be possible, and without them the form cannot be submitted; the telephone number is optional and is only of use if you would rather be called back. Not providing the required data simply means that we cannot act on your enquiry.
  • Security of the website and of the infrastructure (Article 6(1)(f): the legitimate interest of the Data Controller in keeping the service running and protected against abuse). This purpose covers the technical logs automatically recorded by the hosting provider.

How long we keep it

Messages sent through the contact form are kept for a maximum of 12 months from the date they are received, after which they are deleted. Technical infrastructure logs follow the retention periods of the hosting provider indicated below.

Who it may be disclosed to

The data is neither disseminated nor transferred to third parties for commercial purposes. It is processed by the Data Controller's authorised personnel and by the hosting provider, acting as data processor.

Hosting and infrastructure

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (2325 3rd Street, Suite 296, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which provides the web hosting service and the technical handling of the contact forms. Netlify records security logs containing technical data such as the IP address, on the basis of the legitimate interest referred to in Article 6(1)(f) of the Regulation.

Netlify adheres to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as ensuring an adequate level of protection for data transferred to the United States. You may ask us for a copy of the safeguards applied to this transfer by writing to antipapafondi@gmail.com: we will send it to you by email.

Your rights

You may at any time exercise the rights set out in Articles 15 to 22 of the Regulation: to access your data, to request its rectification or erasure, to obtain restriction of processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, to request data portability and, where processing is based on consent, to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, write to antipapafondi@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Withdrawing your consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time and as easily as you gave it: the withdrawal takes effect from that moment onwards and does not make unlawful anything processed beforehand. The cookie described further down is technical and is not based on your consent, so there is no consent to withdraw there: you can nonetheless change the choice you expressed in the banner at any time, using the Manage cookie preferences button at the foot of this page. For a message sent through the form, an email to antipapafondi@gmail.com is enough, and we will delete what you wrote to us.

No automated decision-making

This website carries out no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Article 22 of the Regulation. No automated system produces any assessment about you: the messages that arrive through the form are read by a person.

Minors

This website is not specifically directed at minors and does not knowingly collect data relating to children under the age of fourteen — fourteen being the age set by Article 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code for consent to processing in relation to information society services. If a child under fourteen has written to us, the message must be sent by the holder of parental responsibility: let us know and we will delete the data.

How the banner works

On your first visit a banner appears with two buttons of equal weight, Accept and Decline: neither of them is hidden, made smaller or moved into a second panel. Closing it without choosing counts as declining, and neither scrolling the page nor carrying on browsing counts as consent. Since the only cookie set is the technical one described above, there are no categories to choose between: the choice concerns that cookie and nothing else.

Your choice is remembered for 186 days, a little over six months, after which the banner appears again. Order no. 231 of 10 June 2021 of the Italian Data Protection Authority prohibits repeating the request on every visit and asking for consent again before at least six months have passed.

No third-party content

The typefaces, images, map and every other resource on this website are hosted directly on our own domain: while browsing these pages, your IP address is not transmitted to any external provider. The links to our social profiles and to WhatsApp are ordinary links: they load nothing until you click them, at which point the privacy policies of the respective providers apply.
